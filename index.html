Built for the phone in the truck

  • Today's calls in visit order, with a map link for each address.
  • One tap moves a call along: En Route, On Site, Completed.
  • The tech builds the invoice at the house. They type two words of the price book, tap the item, and adjust the quantity.
  • Photos with labels, checklists, clock in and out, and notes you can dictate.
  • The tech only sees My Day. Prices, settings and the office screens stay with the office.

One company per RouteCool. Nobody shares.

Most field-service software puts every customer in one big database and prices it to cover everybody. RouteCool gives each company its own copy.

Your data stays in its own database

Every job, invoice, photo and note lives in a database that belongs to your company alone, with backups of its own. Nobody else's records are ever mixed in with yours.

You pay for your own storage, at cost

Photos pile up. A shared system has to price for everyone's pile, including the older customers who pay less, so newer customers get overcharged. With RouteCool, your hosting and storage bill is your own: typically $10–30 a month, paid at cost.

Your accounts, your keys

Google Maps, Stripe, Twilio and email connect with your own accounts. When card payments and texting go live, the fees are whatever those companies charge you. We don't add anything on top.

Spec sheet

Everything below ships today and is included in the rate.

Office

Dispatch board
Techs in rows or columns, drag to retime or reassign, and the day's map beside it.
Map back-fill
Drop every unscheduled call on the map and fill a gap near a tech's route.
Workflow board
Open work by stage (Report Needed, Parts Ordered, Ready to Schedule) with cards you drag between columns.
Crews
A lead plus helpers, with the pay split set per job type.
Search
Search by job number, address, or any person's name, phone or email, using two or more words at once.

Money

Price book
Categories, plan-tier prices and CSV import. Raise a whole group of prices with a preview, and undo it in one click.
Upsells
Recommendations are marked Offered, Accepted or Declined. Accepted work bills itself.
Proposals
Good, Better, Best options, side by side.
Receivables
Aging buckets (current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days) and an overdue list that keeps itself current.
Payments
Search by payer, invoice or check number, so you can answer "whose check is this?" fast.

Warranty & financing

Claims
The warranty company and claim number are kept on the job.
Split billing
One visit can have several invoices, like one to the warranty company and one to the resident.
Repayment
Financed jobs sit in their own status, invoiced monthly.
Maintenance plans
Plan members get their own prices, with alerts when a visit is due.

Records

History
Every change on a job is recorded: what it was, what it became, who changed it and when.
Links
Every screen and popup has its own link. Paste it and a coworker sees exactly what you see.
Your brand
Your logo and name on the sign-in page and in the browser tab.
Feedback
Anyone can tap on any screen. It sends a screenshot to the builder, and the answer comes back in the app.
