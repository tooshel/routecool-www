Scope: www.routecool.com home page (index.html). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: owner / office manager of a ~5-person HVAC, plumbing or home-service company comparing against ~$500/mo suites. Action: email demo@routecool.com. Proof: Kappa Air runs on it at kappaair.routecool.com; real app screens captured from synthetic demo data; honest roadmap of what isn't shipped.

Constraints: no invented testimonials, counts or uptime. SMS/email sending, card payments, and QuickBooks are "coming", not live. Price is $199/mo up to 5 users; hosting is typically $10–30/mo at cost; there's no price for more users.

## Direction contract

THESIS: RouteCool is stamped on like the nameplate on your own condenser: one unit, one serial, yours. It refuses the SaaS hero with a laptop screenshot, blue gradient and icon-tile feature grid.

OWN-WORLD: Brushed-aluminum plate ground (#c9ccd1 family) with black-filled etched caps, riveted corners, hairline spec-table rules and field labels (MODEL / SERIAL / RATED). Safety-orange (#f26a1b) caution labels carry the hard truths. Anodized sky-blue (#0ea5e9) plate is the only action color. Typefaces: Archivo (variable width, extra-condensed caps for stamping) and Martian Mono for field values. Flat, with no glow or glass.

STORY: Visitor learns that each company gets its own instance (domain, database, backups), that it pays its own storage at cost, and that the app does the dispatch, phone and invoicing work. Kappa Air runs on it. The visitor emails for a demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim stamped header strip (badge, 4 links, blue "Email for a demo"). Below it, one riveted nameplate fills about 80% of the viewport. The top line reads ROUTECOOL · FIELD SERVICE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM. The etched headline sits on the left at display scale. On the right is a spec table: MODEL, SERIAL = yourcompany.routecool.com (the visitor types to stamp their own), DATABASE dedicated, RATED 5 USERS, RATE $199/MO, HOSTING at cost. Primary action: the anodized blue plate under the table. An orange caution label is riveted at the corner.

FORM: Equipment data plate. It was my top-ranked candidate (#1), taken as the pick card. Seed key c7584ba8. Signature interaction: type your company name and it is punch-stamped into the SERIAL field, and the demo mailto subject updates. Motion grammar: stamp (a short press-in, letter by letter); everything else stays still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
